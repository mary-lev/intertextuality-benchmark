## The finding

The found-manuscript topos in the Introduction is the tradition's common property — Cervantes appears in 20 of 39 other commentaries, Scott in 18, Cuoco in 10, nearly all in the same note. What is Nardi's own is not the list of names but the argument he builds from them, and a handful of references no one else makes.

- **He builds a genealogy.** Boiardo → Ariosto's Turpino → Cervantes → Scott → Manzoni, with Scott demoted to a relay rather than a source.
- **He excludes as carefully as he includes.** Cuoco and Alessandro Verri are named *in order to be ruled out*: their mystifications are serious, Manzoni's is «da burla».
- **He reads forward, not only back.** Rovani, Pirandello, Balzac and Flaubert all postdate the novel. They illuminate Manzoni rather than feed him.

Unique to Nardi across all 40 commentaries: **Rovani, Conde/Rocroi, San Lorenzo**.

## The sharpest result: 26 annotate the passage, 2 hear Dante

Every note carries a Quarantana token anchor, so agreement can be tested at the passage rather than by name. At `c1_12438–12442`, 26 of the 40 commentaries gloss don Abbondio's «testimonio consolante della coscienza». Only Nardi and Bianchi name the source. Viti-Sapegno quotes the same tercet but anchors two tokens earlier (`c1_12424–12426`), so an anchor-only test would have missed it — which is why both tests were run. Everyone else glosses the irony and leaves the borrowing silent.

> **Nardi · cap1-n73** — *il testimonio consolante della coscienza*: quanto piú nascosta, tanto piú mordente, l'ironia del M. Come dice Dante? «… coscienza m'assicura, La buona compagnia che l'uom francheggia, Sotto l'usbergo del sentirsi pura» (*Inferno* XXVIII, 115-7).
>
> **Bianchi · cap1-n55** — *il testimonio consolante della coscienza*: Vengono in mente i versi danteschi (Inf., XXVIII, 115 sgg.) […] a cui **forse** il M. pensò; e diciamo 'forse', perchè è diff…

Bianchi hedges where Nardi does not, and reads «m'**assecura**» against Nardi's «m'**assicura**».

## Why the raw counts lie

- **Obligatory glosses.** *Argo*, *Briareo* and *Parche* score 25–26, but they are words in Manzoni's text — every editor must gloss them. Checked against the Quarantana source, not assumed.
- **The wrong Verri.** 11 raw matches, 2 real. Most cite **Pietro** Verri's *Storia di Milano* and *Osservazioni sulla tortura*. Nardi means **Alessandro** Verri's *Avventure di Saffo*. (Title and date also vary: Nardi 1780 'di Mitilene'; Poggi-Salani 1782; Viti-Sapegno 'di Lesbo'.)
- **The wrong Dante.** 9 raw matches, 2 real. Two are **Dante Isella**, a modern scholar; two more cite unrelated Dante loci.

Ten of the 32 rows are audited. The remaining 22 are usable as leads but should not be quoted as counts without the same check. **Ariosto (12), Galileo (15) and Ripamonti (15)** are the likeliest to behave like Verri and Dante did.
