## Material by others

Texts and quotations from other sources are included only so that the results can
be reproduced; they remain under their own terms and are not covered by the
licences above:

- the poem texts in `data/russian_poetry/gold.csv`, from the PoeTree corpus (Plecháč et
  al. 2025) and, for the ten poems it lacks, the editions named in `referent_text_source`;
- the commentary sentences quoted in `data/russian_poetry/gold.csv` (`commentary`),
  from the critical editions listed in Appendix A of the paper;
- the texts of *Crime and Punishment* (as reproduced by the Russian Virtual
  Library), of the 1823 Russian New Testament and of the Church Slavonic New
  Testament in `data/crime_and_punishment/`;
- the idiom list in `data/russian_poetry/idiom_candidates.csv`, from the Russian
  Wiktionary (CC BY-SA);
- the DanteSources extract and the Biblioteca Italiana texts in `data/dante/source/`;
- the quotations from Nardi's commentary in `data/manzoni/`.
